mpv

1 Settings

The mpv Makefile rule links one of these to mpv/mpv.conf, depending on the host and operating system. They all loop playlists and prefer English subtitles.

🔗
Makefile-mpv

Used by index.org→Makefile.

Other metadata key/value pairs:

  • __block_type = src

  • __src_language = makefile

mpv:
	ln -fns ${C}/mpv ${H}/.config/mpv
ifeq ('${T}','k0')
	ln -fs mpv.${T}.conf mpv/mpv.conf
else ifeq ('${P}','Darwin')
	ln -fs mpv.osx.conf mpv/mpv.conf
else
	ln -fs mpv.linux.conf mpv/mpv.conf
endif

On k0, we also ask for hardware decoding and the gpu video output.
